# First box starts at the top of the window

## User Story

Sam opens dre-flex and the empty box greets him at the top of the window instead of
floating in the middle. He adds a second and third box and they stack downward from the
top, and when he drags the window bigger they stay pinned to the top.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. On launch, the first empty box sits at the top of the window.
2. A second and third box stack below the first, separated by the usual one-line gap.
3. After the terminal window is resized, the boxes are still at the top of the new window.
